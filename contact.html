FAQs

Questions we get a lot

How fresh is the turmeric powder?

We mill in 20 kg batches roughly every ten days rather than milling a year's stock at once. The milling date is stamped on the pouch — if it's more than eight weeks old when it reaches you, tell us and we'll replace it.

What does the curcumin percentage actually mean?

It's the share of the powder made up of curcuminoids, the compounds responsible for turmeric's colour and most of its studied effects. Commodity haldi typically tests around 2%. Our Lakadong runs 6–7.5% depending on the season; each pouch carries the assay for its own batch.

How do you ship fresh corn without it going starchy?

Cobs are cut at dawn with husks left on, pre-cooled within two hours and shipped in insulated liners with gel packs. Sugars start converting to starch immediately after cutting, so we only ship fresh corn to cities we can reach within 48 hours.

Is your corn GMO?

No. All our corn is open-pollinated or traditional heirloom seed, saved and replanted by the growers themselves. GM corn is not approved for cultivation in India, and our organic certification would prohibit it regardless.

What are the delivery charges?

₹49 flat, free above ₹599. Fresh corn ships Monday to Wednesday only so it doesn't sit in a warehouse over the weekend.

Can I return something?

Pantry items: unopened within 7 days, full refund. Fresh produce: if anything arrives in poor condition, send a photo within 24 hours and we'll refund or reship — no return needed.

Do you supply in bulk?

Yes — cafés, cloud kitchens and retailers buy turmeric in 5–25 kg lots and popcorn kernels in 25 kg sacks. Write to trade@farmproducts.example for a rate card.
