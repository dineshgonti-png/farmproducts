Why we bother naming them

Three varieties, three different jobs

For colour and curcumin Lakadong, Rajendra Sonia, Pragati

The high-curcumin group. If you're buying for potency (supplement units, golden-milk blends, anyone quoting a percentage), these are the ones worth paying more for.

For volume and appearance Salem, Rajapuri, BSR-2, NDH

Bold fingers, even drying, dependable yield. This is what moves through the mandi and into masala grinding, bought on look and bulk as much as on assay.

For the uses turmeric doesn't cover Black turmeric, mango ginger, Armoor local

Nalla pasupu and mamidi allam aren't cooking turmeric at all. They go into pickle, medicine and ritual. The Armoor landrace sits apart too: it's what the belt grew before the released varieties came.
