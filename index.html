How it works

Field to kitchen in four steps

Harvested to order

Cobs are cut and rhizomes lifted the morning your order is packed — not weeks before.

Dried & milled slow

Turmeric sun-dries on bamboo racks, then goes through a stone mill that stays cool.

Tested & sealed

Each batch is assayed for curcumin, pesticide residue and aflatoxin before it's sealed.

At your door

Cold-chain for fresh corn, kraft pouches for the rest. Most cities in 48 hours.
